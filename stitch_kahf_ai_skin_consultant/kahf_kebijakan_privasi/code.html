1. Introduction

Welcome to KAHF. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact us at privacy@kahf.com.

When you visit our website and use our services (including the AI Skin Analyzer), you trust us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we describe our privacy policy. We seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Website, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise when you contact us.

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, shipping address.
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, payment methods (processed securely via third-party providers).
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our Routine Builder and digital storefront.

2.1 AI Face Analysis & Camera Data Privacy

Our AI Skin Analyzer requires temporary access to your device's camera or uploaded photos to assess skin conditions and provide personalized Halal skincare recommendations.

Ephemeral Processing

Images are processed in real-time in your browser or immediately deleted from our servers post-analysis. We do not store your facial imagery.

No Biometric Tracking

We extract topographical skin data (e.g., hydration levels, blemishes) purely for product matching. We do not generate or store identifiable biometric profiles.
