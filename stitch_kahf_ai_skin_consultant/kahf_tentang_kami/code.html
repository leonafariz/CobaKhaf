Recognizing a gap in the market for high-quality, ethically sourced men's grooming products that aligned with Islamic values, the concept of KAHF was born in Jakarta.

KAHF officially launched nationwide, rapidly becoming a staple in the routines of modern Indonesian men seeking reliable, nature-inspired care.
